# Elleson Connect 2.2.0

Elleson Connect is the new name of your personal phone and Windows companion. This update includes a new EC logo, Android adaptive launcher icons, Windows application and installer icons, web installation icons, and the included Elleson Keyboard.

## Update

Install **Elleson-Connect-Android-2.2.0.apk** over the existing Android app. Choose **Update**; do not uninstall it first. The package identity and signing certificate are unchanged, so connections, settings and keyboard preferences remain available. The keyboard still appears in Android's keyboard picker, now under **Elleson Keyboard · Elleson Connect**.

The Windows update is **Elleson-Connect-Host-Setup-2.2.0.exe**. Your website address, accounts, paired devices, clipboard history and existing transfer locations remain unchanged.

## Use your PC mouse across the whole phone

1. On the phone, tap **PC mouse on phone** and approve sharing your **Entire screen**.
2. On the PC, open **Phone control**, select the phone and choose **Request phone control**.
3. Approve on the phone and enable **Elleson Connect phone control** in Android Accessibility settings when prompted.
4. Press **Android Home**. The native foreground service keeps capture and input running while you use other phone apps. Stop from its notification when finished.
5. For movement straight across the desktop edge, use **Arrange phone & PC**, choose the phone's position and enable **Move mouse across the screen edge**. Escape returns the mouse to Windows.

Rebranding does not change Android's protected-screen restrictions or permit control after the phone revokes permission. Swiping the app away, signing out or explicitly stopping sharing ends the approved session.

## Google sign-in branding

Your existing Google OAuth client and callback stay valid. Google controls the name on its own consent screen; its Branding page can be set to **Elleson Connect** without changing client credentials. All sign-in screens served by this app already use the new name.
